Glossary

  • Recipe Workload CostThe workload consumed per action. Varies with worker level and perk.
  • ProductionThe workload divided by the recipe's workload cost, rounded down. The number of times a worker can perform the action in a day.
  • Total ProductionProduction with the specialization bonus and tool production modifier applied.
  • Facility MultiplierA fixed multiplier determined per facility (tentative name).
  • Villager RequirementThe amount of demand one unit of the product satisfies. This is 1 when the product itself is the requirement (e.g. guards, priests).
  • Daily ProductionTotal production multiplied by the facility multiplier and the villager requirement — the tool's final output value.

About This Tool

  • This tool doesn't account for tool durability or material shortages, so actual production may fall below the calculated value due to these factors.
  • The coefficients used in the calculation are estimates derived from measured data, so actual results may differ.
  • Terminology is matched to in-game display as closely as possible, but an exact match is not guaranteed.
